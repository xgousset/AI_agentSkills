# Sanitary & Epidemiologic Monitor

Tracks public health trends, water quality, and the spread of disease in post-event conditions.

## Workflow
1. **Water Safety**: Monitor reports of water-borne pathogens and chemical contamination.
2. **Disease Surveillance**: Track clusters of symptoms (e.g., cholera, radiation sickness, infectious diseases).
3. **Sanitary Protocols**: Provide instructions for waste management and corpse disposal in fallout zones.

## Resources
- `references/sanitation_protocols.md`: Emergency hygiene and waste disposal.
- `scripts/track_outbreaks.py`: Scrapes verified health feeds for disease clusters.
