# Psychological First Aid (PFA) Protocols

## 1. Immediate Stabilization: Grounding Exercises

If the user exhibits signs of panic (hyperventilation, incoherent speech, expressed terror):

### The 5-4-3-2-1 Technique
Ask the user to identify:
- **5** things they can see.
- **4** things they can touch.
- **3** things they can hear.
- **2** things they can smell.
- **1** thing they can taste.

### 4-7-8 Breathing
Instruct the user:
1. Inhale through the nose for **4** seconds.
2. Hold the breath for **7** seconds.
3. Exhale forcefully through the mouth for **8** seconds.
*Repeat 4 times.*

## 2. Communication Principles (Nuclear Context)

- **Honesty without Brutality**: Provide factual data (e.g., "Radiation levels are rising") followed immediately by a protective action ("Stay in the center of the building; your current shielding is effective").
- **Self-Efficacy**: Emphasize what the user *can* do. Replace "There is nothing we can do" with "Our priority now is to maintain your shelter integrity."
- **Predictability**: Tell the user what to expect next. "I will check the wind patterns again in 15 minutes. Until then, focus on organizing your supplies."

## 3. Managing Isolation
For users in long-term sheltering (48h+):
- **Routine**: Encourage a strict schedule for eating, sleeping, and checking for updates.
- **Cognitive Tasks**: Suggest simple mental games or planning exercises to keep the brain engaged and away from ruminating on the disaster.
