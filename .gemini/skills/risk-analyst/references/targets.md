# Strategic Target Categorization

## Priority 1: Counter-force (Military)
- ICBM Silos and Command Centers.
- Nuclear Submarine Bases.
- Strategic Bomber Airfields.
- Major Communications Hubs.

## Priority 2: Counter-value (Political/Economic)
- National Capitals and Government Seats.
- Major Industrial Centers.
- Key Transport Hubs (Ports, Railways).
- Energy Infrastructure (Refineries, Power Plants).

## Vulnerability Factors
- **Population Density**: Increases casualty estimates.
- **Topography**: Hills/Mountains can provide shadowing from thermal/blast effects.
- **Infrastructure**: Resilience of local water/power systems.
