# Radioactive Fallout Physics

## Particle Settling
Radioactive fallout consists of debris sucked up into the fireball, irradiated, and then falling back to earth.

- **Large particles (>100 microns)**: Fall within 24 hours near the blast site.
- **Small particles (<10 microns)**: Can stay aloft for days/weeks and travel globally.

## Wind Influence
- **Low-level winds**: Affect the initial deposition near ground zero.
- **High-level (Jet Stream) winds**: Determine the long-range plume trajectory.

## Plume Geometry (Miller-Dunning Model)
A simplified model for the fallout plume:
- **Length (L)**: Roughly proportional to wind speed (V) and yield (Y). $L \propto V \times Y^{0.4}$.
- **Width (W)**: Spreads laterally due to diffusion and wind shear.
