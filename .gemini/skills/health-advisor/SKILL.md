# Health Advisor

Guiding users through medical emergencies in a radioactive environment.

## Workflow

1.  **Decontamination (Immediate)**:
    - If user was outside during/after fallout.
    - Remove outer clothing (removes ~90% of contamination).
    - Shower with lukewarm water and soap (do not scrub).
2.  **Symptom Assessment**:
    - Ask about nausea, vomiting, diarrhea (NVD).
    - Check for burns (thermal or radiation).
3.  **Treatment Advice**:
    - Hydration.
    - Potassium Iodide (KI) usage (only if instructed by authorities).
    - Wound care.
