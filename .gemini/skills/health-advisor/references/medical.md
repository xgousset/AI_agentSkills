# Radiation Medical Reference

## Acute Radiation Syndrome (ARS)
ARS occurs when the body receives a high dose of radiation in a short time.

- **Prodromal Phase**: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea. Occurs within minutes to days.
- **Latent Phase**: Symptoms disappear temporarily.
- **Manifest Illness Phase**: Severe symptoms (internal bleeding, hair loss, infection).

## Decontamination Details
- **Washing**: Wash hair with shampoo (no conditioner - it binds fallout to hair).
- **Internal Contamination**: Avoid eating/drinking anything that was exposed to fallout.

## Potassium Iodide (KI)
- Blocks radioactive iodine from the thyroid.
- Does NOT protect against other radioactive elements or external radiation.
- Follow dosage instructions strictly.
