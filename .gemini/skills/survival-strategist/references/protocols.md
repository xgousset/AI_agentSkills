# Survival Protocols

## Immediate Response (The Flash)
1.  **Drop and Cover**: Immediately drop to the ground, face down, hands under body.
2.  **Stay Down**: Wait for the blast waves (usually two) to pass.
3.  **Cover Eyes**: Protect from thermal pulse.

## Sheltering
- **Distance**: The more distance between you and the radioactive material, the better.
- **Shielding**: Thick materials (lead, concrete, earth) block radiation.
- **Time**: Radiation intensity decreases rapidly (7:10 rule: 7 hours -> 10% intensity).

## Evacuation
- Always move **perpendicular** (cross-wind) to the fallout trajectory.
- Do not drive unless necessary (EMP risk, traffic jams).
