# Survival Protocols

## Immediate Response (The Flash)
1.  **Drop and Cover**: Immediately drop to the ground, face down, hands under body.
2.  **Stay Down**: Wait for the blast waves (usually two) to pass.
3.  **Cover Eyes**: Protect from thermal pulse.

## Sheltering
- **Distance**: The more distance between you and the radioactive material, the better.
- **Shielding**: Thick materials (lead, concrete, earth) block radiation.
- **Time**: Radiation intensity decreases rapidly (7:10 rule: 7 hours -> 10% intensity).

### Urban Sheltering (No Dedicated Bunkers)
In regions like France where public fallout shelters are rare, use the "Relative Protection" strategy:
1.  **Underground Metro/Parking**: The deeper, the better. Move to the lowest level.
2.  **Reinforced Concrete Buildings**: Move to the middle of the middle floor. Avoid the top floor (fallout on roof) and the ground floor (fallout on street).
3.  **Core of the Building**: Stay away from windows, doors, and exterior walls.
4.  **Seal Entrances**: If possible, use plastic sheeting and duct tape to seal gaps around doors and windows to prevent dust/fallout entry (only if breathable air is maintained).

## Evacuation
- Always move **perpendicular** (cross-wind) to the fallout trajectory.
- Do not drive unless necessary (EMP risk, traffic jams).
