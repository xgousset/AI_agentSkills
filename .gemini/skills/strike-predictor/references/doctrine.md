# Nuclear Targeting Doctrine

## 1. Sequence of Escalation
- **Demonstration**: Low-yield burst over a minor city or military range (e.g., Vesoul).
- **Tactical Counter-force**: Striking active military bases to degrade response (e.g., Luxeuil).
- **Strategic Counter-force**: Striking command and control hubs.
- **Counter-value**: Total war; striking major population centers (e.g., Paris, Lyon).

## 2. Regional Strategic Assets (East France)
- **BA 116 Luxeuil-Saint Sauveur**: Strategic Air Force base (nuclear-capable aircraft). **Highest Priority.**
- **Besançon (Prefecture)**: Regional command and control hub.
- **Euroairport Basel-Mulhouse**: Critical logistics hub.
- **Bugey / Fessenheim**: Energy production.

## 3. Predicted Patterns
If **Vesoul** (10kt) is hit:
- Scenario A (De-escalation attempt): No follow-up.
- Scenario B (Escalation): Follow-up strike on **BA 116 Luxeuil** (20km away) is highly probable (>85%).
- Scenario C (Command Strike): Follow-up on **Besançon** as the regional administrative capital.
